About the author

Written by a Counselor Who Understands

Erica Jones Biddings, M.S., Ed.S., is a retired school counselor, author, speaker, and counselor-wellness advocate with more than 30 years of experience. Her books combine real-life insight, practical strategies, and heartfelt encouragement for professionals who support students every day. Mrs. Biddings has trained and served as keynote speaker at many educational conferences and events.

Her one goal and greatest desire is for all school counselors to feel supported, valued, inspired, and empowered to thrive—both personally and professionally.
